The Beginning

Born from
a morning walk.

Newport Custom started in 2021 with a simple frustration: why was it so hard to find clothes that felt as relaxed as a Saturday morning on the Balboa Peninsula, but looked polished enough for a client dinner?

Founder Mara Ellison spent two years working with mills in Como, Brittany, and Osaka before producing the first twelve pieces. Everything sold out in three days.

What we stand for

Our values

01

Material honesty

Only natural fibres — linen, silk, cotton, and wool. Each fabric is traceable from mill to garment. We publish our supplier list annually.

02

Slow production

We produce two collections per year, in limited quantities. No overstock, no clearance sales, no race to the bottom on price or quality.

03

Local finishing

Every garment is hand-finished in our Newport Beach atelier — hand-stitched buttons, French seams on fine fabrics, quality checked by a person, not a machine.

04

Longevity

We offer free repairs for the life of every garment. If a button falls off or a seam opens, bring it in. We'd rather fix it than have you replace it.
