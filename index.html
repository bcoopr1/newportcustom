Our Philosophy

Made for the
long weekend.

Newport Custom was born from a single belief: that your clothes should feel as effortless as a coastal morning. We work directly with heritage mills in Italy, France, and Japan — sourcing only natural fibres with transparent provenance.

Every garment is finished by hand in our Newport Beach atelier, where attention to detail is not a selling point — it's simply how things are done.
